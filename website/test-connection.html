About this measurement

This just estimates how fast this one file downloaded to your device — it is not an official speed test. Things like your Wi-Fi, your device and general network conditions can change the result. To get a steadier number, it downloads the same file three times and uses the middle result, so one lucky or unlucky attempt does not decide everything.

The Palestine and UAE numbers come from the same report, measured the same way, at the end of 2025, so they are fair to compare with each other. But they are national averages, not a match for any one person's connection. Your result is just one test on your device — not proof of what internet access is really like across Palestine or the UAE.
