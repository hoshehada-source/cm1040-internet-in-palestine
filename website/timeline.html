Side note: why the delays?

Palestinian mobile operators license their radio frequencies (spectrum) through arrangements with Israel, and route some traffic through Israeli networks (Reuters, 2026). This is often given as one reason 3G reached the West Bank only in 2018, later than in many other countries.
