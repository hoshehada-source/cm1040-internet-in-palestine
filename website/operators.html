Internet access today

Internet use in Palestine is high: about 88.6% of people were online by early 2024 (DataReportal, 2024). Access has improved in stages — 3G reached the West Bank in 2018, and a 4G upgrade was approved in 2026 (Reuters, 2026).

As more services move online, security also becomes more important. Using HTTPS to encrypt data while it travels is one example of protecting users’ information.
