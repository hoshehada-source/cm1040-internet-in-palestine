Background

Internet access in Palestine developed later in some areas than in many other countries. For example, 3G mobile service reached the West Bank in 2018 (AFP, 2018), and a 4G upgrade was approved in 2026 (Reuters, 2026). One reason often given is that mobile operators license their radio frequencies through arrangements with Israel.

Despite this, internet use is high: about 88.6% of people were online by early 2024 (DataReportal, 2024). The difference in access between areas is an example of what is called the “digital divide” — the gap between those with good access and those without.
