Timeline of the Internet in Palestine

Key moments in internet and mobile access, from the first connections through to the 4G upgrade approved in 2026.

Events

2009

First 3G licence talks

Licensing for 3G is first discussed for the West Bank.

2018

3G switched on

3G finally arrives in the West Bank after a long delay.

2020

Learning moves online

Schools and universities move online during COVID-19.

2026

4G approved

A 4G upgrade is approved for the West Bank.

Side note

Mobile masts carry most of the traffic outside the cities, which is why the arrival of 3G mattered so much when it finally happened.
